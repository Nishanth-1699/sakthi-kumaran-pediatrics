Parent resources

Guidance between visits

Quick references for the questions parents ask most. They don’t replace a consultation. If you are unsure, call the clinic.

    Based on the Indian Academy of Pediatrics schedule. The doctor will confirm the exact vaccines for your child.

    Come in straight away, or call 108, if your child:

    • is under 3 months old with a temperature of 38°C or more
    • is breathing fast, grunting, or the chest pulls in with each breath
    • has a fit (seizure), or is very drowsy and hard to wake
    • has a rash that does not fade when pressed with a glass
    • is not feeding, or has had no wet nappy for 8 hours
    • has had a fever for more than 3 days
    At home, if your child is playing and drinking: light clothing, plenty of fluids or breastfeeds, and fever medicine only at the dose your doctor prescribed.
    2 months
    • Smiles back at you
    • Follows your face with their eyes
    • Lifts head briefly during tummy time
    6 months
    • Rolls over both ways
    • Sits with support and reaches for toys
    • Babbles and turns towards sounds
    12 months
    • Pulls to stand, may take first steps
    • Says “amma” or “appa” with meaning
    • Waves bye-bye; picks up small objects with finger and thumb
    2 years
    • Runs and kicks a ball
    • Uses two-word phrases
    • Points to body parts and pictures
    4 years
    • Hops on one foot
    • Tells simple stories and knows some colours
    • Plays cooperatively with other children

    Every child develops at their own pace. If your child loses a skill they once had, mention it at your next visit.
